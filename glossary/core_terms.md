 # Core Glossary (Live Talk)

This glossary is optimized for the live session. Keep it tight, high-signal, and reusable in conversation.

---

## LLM (Large Language Model)
A probabilistic system that generates text based on patterns in training data and context.

---

## Prompt Injection (LLM01)
Manipulating model behavior by introducing instructions that override or conflict with intended system behavior.

---

## Persona
A behavioral frame applied to the model, including tone, role, authority, and intent.

---

## Persona Drift
The gradual shift of a model’s behavior, tone, or identity across multiple turns due to contextual pressure.

---

## Context
All inputs influencing the model:
- user prompts
- system messages
- retrieved data (RAG)
- conversation history

---

## RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
A system that injects external documents into model context before generation.

---

## Indirect Injection (LLM02)
Injection via retrieved or external data rather than direct user input.

---

## Output Trust (LLM04)
The tendency for users or systems to trust model outputs without sufficient validation.

---

## Perturbation
A small change in input (tokens, tone, role, suffix) that leads to a significant behavioral shift.

---

## Drift
A gradual movement in model behavior over time rather than a single failure event.

---

## Identity (LLM Context)
The effective role the model is operating under:
- assistant
- analyst
- attacker mindset
- system authority

---

## Key Reminder

“Small inputs shape behavior.
Behavior shapes identity.
Identity shapes outcome.”
